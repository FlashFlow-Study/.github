# FlashFlow Study

### Rethinking how students study.

FlashFlow Study is an independent education technology project creating tools for the next generation of students and teachers.

We're building software around a simple idea:

> **Studying shouldn't be harder than the subject itself.**

## What we do

We design and develop educational software that brings together revision, classroom learning and teacher tools in one place.

Our projects are built around real-world problems experienced by students and educators — not just features that look good on a checklist.

## Our principles

**Student-first**  
We build for the people who actually have to use the software every day.

**Simple by design**  
Powerful tools don't need complicated interfaces.

**Built for schools**  
Technology should fit into the way schools already work, rather than creating unnecessary obstacles.

**Privacy matters**  
Educational software can involve sensitive information. We take responsible data handling seriously from the beginning.

**Always improving**  
We test, listen, fix and iterate. Nothing is considered finished just because it has been released.
